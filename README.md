A one-line family scene, warm, funny and heartfelt — a dad who framed his daughter's first burnt pancake like a diploma, and a mum who still serves it every Sunday, slightly less burnt, always with applause. `main.ts` writes the scene to `entry.txt` (179 characters, inside the 1–280 gate) and copies the matching illustration `scene.png` beside it. No network, no randomness, no clock — the same run always produces the same two files.

![The family scene: a girl holds up her trophy and certificate while her parents hug her and her little brother reaches for the trophy](scene.png)

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
